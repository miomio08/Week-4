The sphere started on the ground, and moving it upward represents the progress I've made in my life. I've compared this to the character Miles Morales from Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse because his story also focuses on being a better you and improving yourself.

I am comparing this sphere with the animated film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse because, in that movie, the protagonist learns what it means to become his own individual instead of living by what others want from him. He grows from the beginning of the film to the end, and I am trying to visualize that growth through the sphere. As the sphere moves upward, it represents how a person can grow and progress throughout their life.
